Course

About the Program

Generative AI for Work & Research is an intensive 8-week live cohort course focused on No-Code AI tools for responsible automation and research enhancement.

Offered by The GRAPH Courses at the University of Geneva, Switzerland — combining academic rigor with practical, industry-relevant application of cutting-edge AI technologies.

Curriculum

Course Modules

W1 Documents, Presentations & Websites

How LLMs are trained • Prompting principles • Generating DOCX/PPTX/HTML • Building simple websites • AI detection tools

W2 AI-Automated Data Analysis

Analyzing quantitative data responsibly • Coding assistants • Data agents • Verification strategies for statistical outputs

W3 GenAI for Text Processing & Research

Processing hundreds of documents • Structured data extraction • AI literature review tools • Qualitative analysis at scale

W4 Custom GPTs and Chatbots

Building custom knowledge base chatbots • Context engineering • RAG systems • Custom GPTs • Safe chatbot deployment

W5 AI Tools for Workflow Automation

LLM-powered automations • Action & frequency triggers • Research digests • Keeping humans in the loop • Browser agents

W6 GenAI for Communication & Outreach

Image generation models • HTML/SVG infographics • Visual research abstracts • AI video translation & dubbing

W7 Capstone Project & Showcase

Collaborative AI solution development • Peer feedback • Live project demos • Demonstrating responsible AI practice
