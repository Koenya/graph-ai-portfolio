Course Info

About the Course

Generative AI for Work & Research is an 8-week live cohort course focused on No-Code AI tools for responsible task automation.

Offered by The GRAPH Courses at the University of Geneva, Switzerland, combining academic rigor with practical, industry-relevant skills.

📚
Curriculum

Course Modules

Module 1 Documents, Presentations & Websites

How LLMs are trained, prompting principles, generating DOCX/PPTX/HTML, simple websites, and AI detection tools.

Module 2 AI-Automated Data Analysis

Analyzing quantitative data responsibly with coding assistants, data agents, and verification strategies for statistical outputs.

Module 3 GenAI for Text Processing & Research

Processing hundreds of documents at once, structured data extraction, AI literature review tools, and qualitative analysis.

Module 4 Custom GPTs and Chatbots

Building custom knowledge base chatbots, context engineering, RAG, custom GPTs, and safe chatbot deployment.

Module 5 AI Tools for Workflow Automation

LLM-powered automations, action/frequency triggers, research digests, keeping humans in the loop, and browser agents.

Module 6 GenAI for Communication & Outreach

Image generation models, producing HTML/SVG infographics, visual research abstracts, and AI video translation/dubbing.

Module 7 Capstone Project & Showcase

Collaborative development of a final AI solution, peer feedback, live project demos, and demonstrating responsible AI practice.
